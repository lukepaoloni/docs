---
title: "Rates With the Same Name"
description: "What happens when an imported rate has the same name as a rate you created in Shopify."
---

On a store using [market-driven shipping](/market-driven-shipping/overview), your imported rates are offered beside the rates you created in Shopify, so two rates in one market can end up with the same name.

## The Same name badge

MatrixRates marks both rates with a `Same name` badge when an imported rate and a Shopify rate in the same market share a name. Upper and lower case don't matter, so `Standard` and `standard` match.

> Buyers may see two rates with the same name at checkout.

This is a warning, and you can still import. To clear it, rename one of the two rates: change the `Name` column in your file and import again, or rename the rate in Shopify.

## When an import is blocked

MatrixRates applies `Postcode` and `City` values, including `*`, by hiding a rate, by name, from buyers whose address doesn't match. A Shopify rate with exactly the same name, capital letters included, would be hidden from those buyers too, and they could be left with no shipping rate at all.

When the import preview finds this, it marks the Shopify rate `Hidden by MatrixRates rule` and switches the **Import rates** button off. Rename one of the two rates, then upload your file again.

<Warning>
  The check needs your markets from Shopify. If the preview says it couldn't check your markets, or that some Shopify rates couldn't be loaded, compare the names yourself before importing.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The same badge can appear on the Markets page if you create a same-named Shopify rate after importing. The fix is the same.
</Note>
