---
title: "Your Rates After Uninstalling"
description: "On stores using market-driven shipping, imported rates stay live after you uninstall MatrixRates. Here's how to switch them off."
---

This page applies to stores that use [market-driven shipping](/market-driven-shipping/overview).

<Warning>
  Uninstalling MatrixRates does not remove the rates you imported. They stay in your markets and buyers keep seeing them at checkout until you switch them off.
</Warning>

## Before you uninstall

- **Keep a copy of your rates.** On the Growth and Scale plans, [export your rates](/market-driven-shipping/overview#exporting-rates) so you have the file.
- **Decide whether you want the rates to stay live.** If you do, there's nothing else to do. If you don't, switch them off in Shopify as described below.

## Switching your imported rates off

You switch MatrixRates rates on and off in Shopify, market by market. The best time to do this is before you uninstall.

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** > **Markets** and open a market.
2. Open the market's shipping settings. Your MatrixRates rates are listed there alongside the rates you created yourself.
3. Switch off each MatrixRates rate you no longer want to offer. Shopify shows a switched-off rate as inactive, and buyers no longer see it at checkout.
4. Repeat for each market that has MatrixRates rates.

<Note>
  You can switch MatrixRates rates on or off in Shopify, but you can't edit their names or prices there.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Switching shipping off for a whole market stops **every** rate in that market, including the rates you created yourself. Buyers in that market won't be able to check out.
</Warning>

## Changing your rates later

To change your imported rates, install MatrixRates again and import a new file. The app picks up the rates from your earlier install, so the new import replaces them.

<Note>
  Importing again switches every imported rate back on, including any you switched off in Shopify.
</Note>
