---
title: "Your Rates After Uninstalling"
description: "On stores using market-driven shipping, imported rates can stay live after you uninstall MatrixRates. Here's how to switch them off."
---

<Warning>
  On a store using [market-driven shipping](/market-driven-shipping/overview), uninstalling MatrixRates does not remove the rates you imported. They can keep showing at checkout, so switch off any you no longer want before you uninstall.
</Warning>

## Switching your imported rates off

On the Growth and Scale plans, [export your rates](/export-guide) first so you keep a copy.

1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Markets** and click a market.
2. Click **Shipping**.
3. Deactivate each MatrixRates rate you no longer want to offer, then save.
4. Repeat for each market that has MatrixRates rates.

Then test checkout with an address in each market to confirm the rates you expect are offered.

<Warning>
  Switching shipping off for a whole market stops every rate in it, including the ones you created yourself.
</Warning>

Postcode and city restrictions are applied by the app. If you keep a rate that uses them, test checkout with an address inside and outside the area after you uninstall.

## Changing your rates later

You can't edit MatrixRates rates in Shopify. To change them, install MatrixRates again, check that the Markets page shows your existing rates, then import a file with every rate you want to keep. Rates created by an import start switched on.
