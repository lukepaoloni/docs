---

## What's different

- **One import and one export for the whole store.** You don't pick a delivery profile or a location. One file covers every market and every location you ship from.
- **MatrixRates provides your imported rates to Shopify.** They show at checkout beside any rates you created yourself in Shopify. Importing never changes the rates you created in Shopify.
- **Importing replaces all MatrixRates rates in every market.** Your file is the full list of MatrixRates rates, not an addition to what's already there, so you change a rate by editing your file and importing again. In Shopify admin you can switch a MatrixRates rate on or off, but you can't edit it there.
- **Your spreadsheet stays the same.** The columns in the [Spreadsheet Reference](/spreadsheet-reference) are unchanged, and the `Zone` column is still required.

<Note>
  You can switch a MatrixRates rate off in Shopify, but that doesn't survive an import. An import replaces every MatrixRates rate with a new one, and new rates start switched on. If you had switched any off, switch them off again after importing.
</Note>

Shopify's Help Center explains [how market-driven shipping works](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/shipping-options/understanding-shipping-options-by-market), including how rates combine at checkout.

## The Markets page

The **Markets** card lists your markets, with the countries each one covers. Click a market's name to open it. An open market shows two lists:

- **Shopify rates** are the rates you created in Shopify for that market. They are shown for reference only. A carrier rate shows as `Calculated at checkout` instead of a price.
- **MatrixRates rates** are the rates you imported that ship to that market.

A rate appears under every market whose countries it covers. On a market that covers a whole country, a rate limited to part of it has a note after its name, such as `Standard · Amazonas only` or `Standard · rest of Brazil`. On a market for that part of the country alone, the same rate shows as just `Standard`.

### What the badges mean

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `2 Shopify` | The number of different active rate names that you created in Shopify for this market. |
| `3 MatrixRates` | The number of different imported rate names that ship to this market. Rows that share a name count once. |
| `Same name` | An imported rate has the same name as one of your Shopify rates in this market. See [Rates With the Same Name](/market-driven-shipping/same-name-rates). |
| `Hidden by MatrixRates rule` | A postcode or city rule on an imported rate also hides one of your Shopify rates. See [Rates With the Same Name](/market-driven-shipping/same-name-rates#when-an-import-is-blocked). |
| `Not shipping` | Shipping is switched off for this market in Shopify. Your MatrixRates rates aren't offered there either, and the list is headed **MatrixRates rates (not offered)**. |
| `Draft` | The market is a draft in Shopify, so its rates aren't live yet. |
| `Inactive` | The Shopify rate is switched off. The app doesn't show this for imported rates, so check those in Shopify. |
| `Not sellable` | Shown on the **No market** group. See below. |

### The No market group

If your file has rates for a country that isn't in any of your markets, those rates appear in a **No market** group at the bottom of the list with a `Not sellable` badge. The app tells you:

> Customers in these countries can't check out because they aren't in any active market. Add them to a market in Shopify admin under Settings → Markets.

## Importing and exporting

The steps are the same as in the [Import Guide](/import-guide) and [Export Guide](/export-guide), with two differences:

- **There is no delivery profile or location to choose.** The **Import Rates** and **Export Rates** buttons are on the app's home page, in the **Your shipping rates** card. Skip the steps that ask you to select a delivery profile and find a location.
- **An export contains your MatrixRates rates only.** The rates you created in Shopify aren't in the file, so an export is not a backup of those.

<Warning>
  Before you import again, keep a copy of your current rates. On Growth or Scale, [export them](/export-guide). On other plans, keep your last complete file.
</Warning>
